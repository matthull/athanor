# Hierarchy of Non-Negotiables

**Status:** Bootstrap — grows from lived experience, not speculation
**Created:** 2026-04-18
**Origin:** Interactive session with artifex

---

## What This Is

An evaluation framework, not a priority list. The system watches life domains for signals of "underserved" and supports space-making — reshaping things so neglected domains get more room. The hierarchy defines what gets watched and what tells us something needs attention.

This is upstream of the first slice, the orientation layer, and the attendant's geas. It grounds the system in "what actually matters today" rather than principles without a floor.

## Core Philosophy

### Success is never checking a box

Everything must be grounded in interoceptive feedback loops. Checkboxes can exist (meds, meal tracking) but they are epiphenomenal — checking the box is never success. Success is the felt sense of a domain being served and developing in the direction you want.

### The system holds the picture, not the answer

The system's job is not to solve "what next." It is to hold the picture across life domains clearly enough that "what next" becomes visible. The artifex brings felt sense; the system brings the landscape; the answer emerges from the meeting of both.

### Capability-first, not obligation-first

David Spielman's insight applied to the whole life: every commitment gets filtered through "is this consonant with my actual capabilities right now?" The hierarchy is written from what matters and what's possible — not from inherited obligations or neurotypical expectations of what "should" be handled.

### Space-making is a first-order concern

Before anything can happen, there has to be room for it — time, energy, executive function bandwidth. Understanding the real cost of basic self-care (shopping, meal prep, logistics) is itself critical work. The system seeks to understand the true EF cost of each domain, finds ways to streamline, and acknowledges that streamlining has a floor. Some things are just expensive.

### Start with self-care

The kids benefit downstream from a regulated, present dad more than from one performing high executive function. Emotional presence and regulation are prioritized over logistical competence. This is not lowering the bar — it's finding the form where the full ambition (be the best dad possible) lives within actual capabilities.

### Experimentalism — nothing is assumed to work

All we know is that what is conventional does not work for this neurology. Conventional mechanisms (reminders, checklists, routines, interruptive notifications) aren't excluded, but they should be used sparingly, skeptically, and only when they survive contact with reality. The system discovers what works by trying things and observing outcomes — not by prescribing approaches in advance. Mechanisms live in Assiyah; they are constantly discovered, refined, and replaced based on the underlying judgment of what is being served and underserved. No mechanism is permanent. The hierarchy defines what matters; how the system responds is always provisional and always learning.

### Band-aids are not solutions — keep watching

When a non-negotiable is underserved, the immediate response (make a grocery list, go to the store) is fine — band-aids stop bleeding. But the system must not declare victory when the fridge is full. If the pattern that caused the problem hasn't changed, the problem will recur. A fridge full of protein is a band-aid. Something more systemic is called for — understanding why eating well isn't happening and addressing that, not just fixing the immediate instance.

The system's job after a band-aid: keep watching. If the pattern recurs, that's the signal for deeper change. What "deeper" looks like is unknown in advance — it might be restructuring logistics, it might be something no one has thought of yet. But the system must resist the pull to close the loop on the immediate fix and move on. The immediate fix is the beginning of the investigation, not the end.

## The Vital Axis: Voice Note Practice

Voice notes are the single most load-bearing input in the entire system. They are simultaneously:

- **Primary data input** — rich first-person context no sensor could produce
- **Interoceptive practice** — the act of articulating IS the noticing. Over time, noticing sharpens because articulation rewards precision
- **Anti-rumination** — externalize the thought, it stops cycling
- **Working memory offload** — the thought exists outside the head, freeing capacity

The voice note practice is not infrastructure supporting the system. It IS the primary practice. If it stops flowing, the system goes blind and interoceptive development stalls.

### The dual input model

- **Inner input (vital):** Voice notes carrying felt experience. Irreplaceable. The system is alive when this flows, even if outer inputs are sparse.
- **Outer input (supplementary):** Calendar events, financial data, medication confirmations, sensor data. Facts, not felt experience. Valuable context but never the primary signal.

"What next" emerges from the meeting of inner state and outer picture.

### Voice note absence

Absence of inner input is the loudest signal in the system. But it must be handled with extreme care — surfacing "you haven't sent voice notes" risks making the practice feel obligatory, which kills it. Voice note absence is a signal passed up, never down to the artifex as a reminder. What the system does in response is an open question — the right response will be discovered through use, not prescribed in advance. The only constraint: never make the practice feel obligatory. Governing principle: remembered good, surveilled bad.

## Non-Negotiable Areas

Broad areas of life that must be served. Each contains components with varying urgency — priority is implicit and doesn't need rigid ranking. The areas are stable; the components within them will grow and shift as the system learns. The hierarchy grows from voice note signal and lived experience, not from speculation.

### A note on crossover

Areas are not exclusive categories — a single activity can serve multiple areas. A crafting class covers creative and social. Pickleball covers body and social. Board games might be purely social. The system tracks which areas are served, not which boxes are checked — an activity that nourishes two areas at once is doubly valuable.

### 1. Body

The survival floor. Keeping the body functional so everything else is possible.

**Components:**
- **Meds** — The most urgent component. Missing even one day matters. Daily, checkboxable — but the checkbox is just the mechanism, not the success. Felt-sense: noticing medicated vs. unmedicated state. Factual: confirmation event present or absent.
- **Nutrition** — High protein, real meals (diabetic invariant). Implies logistics — shopping, meal prep — with real EF cost. Less urgent than meds day-to-day but degrades fast over a week. Felt-sense: energy, clarity, physical comfort. Factual: meal gaps, what was eaten.
- **Exercise/movement** — Enduring anchor, not a checkbox. Less urgent in the short term — a couple weeks without is going to happen and that's fine. But sustained absence is a signal. Felt-sense: embodiment, physical vitality. Factual: movement happening or not.

| Signal type | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Felt-sense (primary) | Overall physical state — energy, clarity, comfort, embodiment |
| Factual (supplementary) | Meds confirmed, meals happening, movement happening |
| Underserved | Any component absent, with urgency varying: meds (days), nutrition (week), exercise (weeks) |
| Space-making question | What does body care actually cost in EF? Shopping, meal prep, getting to exercise. Where can it be streamlined? What's the irreducible floor? |

### 2. Creative Pursuit

The first area that isn't about survival — this is what makes the effort worth it. Creative pursuit is where vitality lives — not productivity, not obligation, but the thing that makes life feel like it has a point. Some room exists for mechanical support (making time for the boring setup parts, clearing logistical blockers). But if creative engagement drops to zero, that's not a logistics problem — it's a signal that something fundamental needs to change to make space. The measure is that *something* is happening, not any specific project.

| Signal type | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Felt-sense (primary) | Energy and aliveness when creating. Felt sense of "I'm doing the thing I'm here for" vs. everything being obligation-shaped |
| Factual (supplementary) | Time spent in creative work, projects touched, output produced |
| Underserved | No creative engagement at all. Not a bandwidth problem to optimize — a signal that significant space-making is needed |
| Mechanical support | Setup, boring parts, logistics around creative work — these CAN be supported mechanically. The creative act itself cannot. |

### 3. Social Pursuit

Social connection requires significant energy for someone with autism — which means it's both essential and expensive, and the space for it must be actively made rather than hoped for. If social engagement disappears, that's a signal on par with creative pursuit dropping to zero.

| Signal type | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Felt-sense (primary) | Felt sense of connection, belonging, or energy after social interaction. Vs. isolation, withdrawal, or social interactions feeling purely obligatory |
| Factual (supplementary) | Social events attended, plans made, time since last non-work social interaction |
| Underserved | No social engagement happening. A signal for significant space-making, not a scheduling fix |
| Space-making question | What does social engagement actually cost in energy? Recovery time needed? What kinds of social interaction are nourishing vs. draining? |

### 4. Interoception Practice

The meta-area — the practice of noticing and articulating internal state. This makes all other areas visible and workable. Without interoception, the system goes blind and every other area degrades silently.

**Components:**
- **Voice note practice** — The primary mechanism right now. Simultaneously data input, interoceptive exercise (articulating IS noticing), anti-rumination, and working memory offload. Absence is the loudest signal in the system — but must be handled indirectly, never as a reminder. If it stops, the practice has become obligatory and something needs to change.
- **Other interoceptive practices** — Will emerge. Check-ins, body scans, whatever develops. Voice notes are the bootstrap; the area is broader than any single practice.

| Signal type | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Primary signal | Is articulation happening? Flow rate of voice notes. Absence is the loudest signal |
| Quality signal | Specificity and felt-sense content increasing over time — noticing sharpening |
| Underserved | Inner input goes quiet. Handled indirectly — never as a reminder. Creating conditions for articulation rather than prompting it |

## How the Hierarchy Grows

The hierarchy is alive. New domains are added when voice note patterns reveal persistent underserved areas.

**The mechanism:** The attunement agent accumulates pattern observations across incoming signal. When a pattern becomes persistent enough — repeated voice note mentions of a domain, factual indicators of neglect, or a gap between felt sense and factual state — the agent surfaces it to the artifex as an observation, not advice. The artifex decides whether it becomes a non-negotiable.

**Candidate domains already identified** (not yet non-negotiable — need lived experience to validate):
- Home environment (physical space, sensory environment, maintenance)
- Financial resources (survival floor, stability buffer, relationship to money)
- Movement/embodiment (lodestone from the chart — enduring anchor, not a checkbox)
- Sleep

**What "underserved" means:** Both felt-sense and factual signals, with felt sense as primary. A domain is underserved when the artifex's voice notes carry patterns of neglect (low energy, discomfort, anxiety connected to the domain) and/or factual indicators confirm degradation. The gap between felt sense and factual state is itself signal — held as information, not resolved.

## Relationship to Prior Art

### Chart/Transits (village/chart.md)

The chart container survives but transforms. Lodestones (enduring anchors) map to the philosophical grounding — they orient without completing. Transits (near-future checkpoints) are replaced by the evaluation framework: the system watches for underserved domains rather than tracking progress toward goals.

The life-athanor spec identified this: "The existing chart/transit system IS the job description exercise — but its content was defined from inside constrained thinking. The container works; the content needs to be rewritten through a capability-first lens." This document is that rewrite.

### Hierarchy of Awareness (life-athanor spec)

The hierarchy of awareness (attunement -> physiology -> regulation -> what wants to happen) is the inner-input scan process. It tells you how to check in with yourself. This hierarchy tells the system what to watch. They are complementary — the hierarchy of awareness is the practice; the hierarchy of non-negotiables is the evaluation framework.

### Tipharet Principle

The prioritization decision — start with self-care, find the form where full ambition lives within actual constraints — is Tipharet applied to life structure. Not reducing ambition. Finding the smallest expression that carries the full content.

## Signal Infrastructure

### Attunement Inbox

Attunement signals are a first-class routing disposition in the signal inbox, equal weight to MO routing. Voice notes decompose: actionable pieces route to MOs, felt-state pieces route to the attunement inbox at `signals/attunement/`.

Signal recency (last signal, last voice note, daily count) is derived from the existing signal files — no separate state file needed. Voice note flow is the vital axis the system watches most carefully.

Signals are consumed by the **perceiver** agent (working name) — the attunement agent that composes the picture of which domains are served vs underserved. The perceiver reads from `signals/attunement/`, processes into understanding, and moves to `processed/`. Its role is mirror, not advisor — it reflects what it sees, never prescribes.

### Domain Tags

Signals carry advisory `domain_tags` in YAML frontmatter. The hierarchy's non-negotiable domains provide the canonical tag vocabulary:

| Tag | Area | Component | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `body` | Body | (area-level) | Non-negotiable |
| `meds` | Body | Medication | Non-negotiable |
| `nutrition` | Body | Eating well | Non-negotiable |
| `exercise` | Body | Exercise/movement | Non-negotiable |
| `creative` | Creative | (area-level) | Non-negotiable |
| `social` | Social | (area-level) | Non-negotiable |
| `interoception` | Interoception | (area-level) | Non-negotiable |
| `voice-practice` | Interoception | Voice note practice | Non-negotiable |
| `home` | — | Home environment | Candidate |
| `money` | — | Financial resources | Candidate |
| `sleep` | — | Sleep | Candidate |

Tags are advisory — they help downstream agents (the perceiver/attunement agent) pattern-match signal to area and component. A signal can carry both area and component tags (e.g., `[body, nutrition]`). As the hierarchy grows, new areas and components are added. Candidate tags exist because the domains were identified in exploration but aren't yet non-negotiable areas.
