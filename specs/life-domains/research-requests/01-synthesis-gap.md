# Research Request: The Synthesis Gap

**Requested:** 2026-04-20
**Requester:** life-domains MO, athanor-architect
**Purpose:** Prior art survey — identify whether the integrated system we're building has precedent, or whether existing work stops short of full synthesis

---

## Research Question

What existing frameworks, systems, or bodies of practice attempt to synthesize interoceptive awareness, executive function support, and neurodivergent-specific life management into a coherent daily operating system? Not individual techniques in isolation, but *integrated approaches* that combine multiple of these elements into something a person lives from day to day.

## Why This Matters

We are building a system (the "life athanor") that combines:
- **Interoceptive awareness as the primary practice** — every decision starts with "how am I actually doing right now?" not "what's on my list"
- **Executive function infrastructure** — an agent system that carries logistics, follow-through, and coordination so the person can afford to pause for attunement
- **Neurodivergent-specific design** — capability fluctuation as the core reality (not a limitation to work around), stimulation matching instead of rest, autopilot as conscious resource allocation, materia-not-tasks framing
- **Radical non-negotiables** — if a responsibility isn't compatible with your neurology *right now*, the answer is doing something different, not forcing fit. This goes beyond burnout-recovery boundary-setting to a fundamental reorientation of agency
- **A "cognitive prosthesis" framing** — not a productivity tool, not a therapy app, but an extension of the person's own awareness and executive capacity

The individual ingredients all exist in various fields. The question is whether anyone has synthesized them into an integrated system — and if not, where existing work stops short and why.

## Specific Areas to Investigate

### 1. Neurodivergent life management frameworks
- Beyond ADHD coaching and CBT-for-ADHD — are there holistic frameworks that address daily living (not just work productivity)?
- Frameworks that center fluctuating capability rather than treating it as a problem to solve
- Anything that frames the system around "consonance with actual neurology" rather than "compensation for deficits"

### 2. Integrated interoception + executive function approaches
- Clinical programs that combine body-awareness training with practical life management
- OT approaches that bridge the gap between "learning to notice your body" and "living your life differently because you notice"
- Any system where interoceptive check-in is the *entry point* for decision-making, not a parallel wellness practice

### 3. The "operating system for daily life" concept
- Has anyone explicitly framed a personal management system as an "operating system" with architectural rigor?
- Digital or analog systems that go beyond task management to encompass the full loop: awareness → state assessment → capability matching → action selection → follow-through
- Systems thinking or cybernetics-influenced approaches to personal self-regulation

### 4. Where existing work stops short
- Burnout recovery: boundary-setting without the radical non-negotiables framing — does anyone take it further?
- ADHD coaching: executive function support without interoceptive integration — who bridges the gap?
- Mindfulness/somatic practices: awareness without the infrastructure to act on it — who adds the infrastructure?
- Productivity systems (GTD, bullet journal, etc.): structure without neurodivergent-specific design — any notable attempts to adapt these?

## Context for the Researcher

The person building this system has ADHD and autism. The system originated from a psychiatrist's reframe ("if it's not compatible with your neurology, do something different") and has been self-built over several months into a comprehensive agent-based system. The core insight: the individual techniques (interoception, executive function support, non-negotiables, attunement) each exist, but the *synthesis into a coherent daily operating system* is what has been transformative. The research question is whether this synthesis exists elsewhere — in clinical practice, academic research, consumer products, or informal community practices.

Key documents for reference if needed:
- The life-athanor spec articulates the full philosophy (attunement-centered, capability-first, materia-not-tasks, rhythm-not-routine)
- The synthesis document describes the technical architecture (perceiver/attendant agents, three work shapes, orientation layer)
- The hierarchy of awareness: attunement → physiological basics → regulation → what wants to happen

## Desired Output

A structured survey of prior art organized by proximity to our synthesis. For each significant finding:
- What it is and who created it
- Which elements of our synthesis it covers
- Where it stops short or diverges
- Key citations or references for deeper investigation

Conclude with an honest assessment: is the synthesis genuinely novel, well-trodden ground we haven't found yet, or somewhere in between?
