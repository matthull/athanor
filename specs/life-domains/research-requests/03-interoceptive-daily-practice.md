# Research Request: Interoceptive Practice as Daily Operating System

**Requested:** 2026-04-20
**Requester:** life-domains MO, athanor-architect
**Purpose:** Prior art survey — investigate who is working on interoception as a continuous daily practice rather than a clinical intervention, and what "living from interoception" looks like as a lifestyle

---

## Research Question

Beyond Kelly Mahler's interoception curriculum (which is clinically framed), who is working on interoceptive awareness as a *continuous daily practice* — something you live from, not something you do in a therapy session? What does the bridge look like between "I learned to notice my body signals" and "my daily life is organized around noticing my body signals first"?

## Why This Matters

The system we're building has interoceptive awareness as its literal entry point. The hierarchy of awareness is:

1. **Attunement** — Am I actually checked in with myself right now, or on autopilot?
2. **Physiological basics** — Water, food, pain, sleep, meds (signals that are unreliable in neurodivergent people)
3. **Regulation** — Am I in a state where I can make choices?
4. **What wants to happen** — Only now does "what should I do" become meaningful

This isn't a wellness layer bolted onto a task manager. It's the architecture. You don't see your tasks until you've checked in with your body. The question: who else has taken interoception this far — from clinical technique to life architecture?

## Specific Areas to Investigate

### 1. Kelly Mahler and the interoception curriculum
- What exists beyond the clinical/educational curriculum? Has Mahler or her community extended into daily-life application?
- How do OTs trained in Mahler's approach help clients integrate interoception into daily living (not just session work)?
- What does the research say about transfer — do people who learn interoceptive awareness in clinical settings actually use it in daily life?

### 2. Polyvagal-informed daily practices
- Deb Dana's work on polyvagal theory in everyday life — how does this translate to daily practice?
- The "ventral vagal" community — practices for maintaining regulatory state throughout the day
- Stephen Porges' work and its practical applications beyond clinical settings
- Polyvagal-informed coaching or life design

### 3. Somatic practices as daily operating systems
- Somatic Experiencing (Peter Levine) — anyone extending this beyond trauma processing into daily self-regulation?
- Feldenkrais, Alexander Technique — as daily awareness practices, not just movement sessions
- Body-based coaching methodologies (e.g., Strozzi Institute's somatic coaching)
- Focusing (Eugene Gendlin) as a daily decision-making practice, not just a therapy technique

### 4. Constructed emotion / body-budget approaches
- Lisa Feldman Barrett's body-budget concept — has anyone built a practical daily system around it?
- "How Emotions Are Made" applied: treating the body budget as the primary input for daily planning
- Allostasis-informed lifestyle design

### 5. Contemplative and indigenous traditions
- Mindfulness traditions that specifically emphasize body awareness as the primary practice (not concentration/focus)
- Yoga traditions centered on interoception (pratyahara, but practically applied to daily life)
- Any tradition where "check in with your body before acting" is the core discipline
- Indigenous wellness practices that center body awareness in daily living

### 6. The neurodivergent-specific dimension
- Interoceptive differences in ADHD and autism are well-documented clinically — but who is developing *practices* specifically for people whose interoceptive signals are unreliable?
- The paradox: the people who most need interoceptive awareness are the people for whom it's hardest. Who is working on this specific problem?
- Alexithymia-informed approaches to building interoceptive vocabulary
- The role of external supports (other people, technology, environment) in compensating for unreliable interoception

### 7. The "practice" dimension specifically
- What does a sustainable daily interoceptive practice look like — not a 30-day program but a lifelong operating mode?
- How do practices avoid becoming rote (the attunement check itself becoming autopilot)?
- What keeps practices alive over months and years? What makes them decay?
- The role of a practice partner, coach, or (in our case) AI agent in sustaining practice

## Context for the Researcher

A key insight driving this system: for neurodivergent people, interoceptive signals are unreliable. You might not feel hungry when you're hungry. You might not feel tired when you're exhausted. The body signals neurotypical people use to self-regulate are muted or delayed.

This means interoceptive *practice* for neurodivergent people can't just be "listen to your body" — because the body doesn't always speak clearly. The system we're building uses a perceiver agent that builds a picture of the person's state from multiple signals (voice notes, time patterns, sensor data) and holds that picture even when the person's own interoception is offline.

The question: is anyone else working on this specific problem — interoceptive practice for people with unreliable interoception? And more broadly, who is taking interoception from clinical technique to daily life architecture?

The person behind this system has been transformed by this practice shift — going from obligation-driven to attunement-driven daily life. The catalyst was a single psychiatrist session. The bootstrapping happened through self-directed system building. This raises the question: is the practice itself the therapeutic agent, and the technology just scaffolding?

## Desired Output

A survey organized by domain (OT, somatic, polyvagal, contemplative, neurodivergent-specific). For each significant practitioner, framework, or body of work:
- What they teach and how it's practiced
- Whether it extends into daily life or stays clinical/sessional
- How it handles the neurodivergent interoception paradox (if at all)
- Key works, papers, or resources for deeper investigation

Conclude with: where is the frontier of "interoception as daily operating system"? Who is closest to what we're building, and what's the remaining gap?
