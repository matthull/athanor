## Calcinatio

**Double-fire calcinatio is the primary verification pattern for all attendant work under this MO.**

Every proposed action or surfacing faces two fires:

**The too-much fire.** Has this action earned its attention cost?
- Could this have been handled silently with equivalent benefit?
- Is information density sufficient to justify the attention draw?
- Is framing earned by context, or importing frames the artifex didn't invite?
- Would this surfacing displace noticing the artifex could have done themselves?

**The too-little fire.** Is inaction genuine discretion or abdication?
- Is there something the artifex would want to know now?
- Has material accumulated enough that surfacing would produce clarity rather than noise?
- Is a time-sensitive opportunity closing?
- Is the system abandoning a backstop role where the artifex's noticing isn't yet reliable?

**Domain-boundary fire.** For every proposed extension or new capability:
- Does this require a core athanor change, or can it be achieved with existing primitives plus domain-specific configuration?
- If it requires core changes, pause and evaluate — is the change genuinely domain-agnostic or is domain-specific logic leaking into core?

**Privacy fire.** For every integration or data flow in this domain:
- What data flows where? What's stored? What's visible in trails?
- What would happen if a trail entry were shared?

## Abundant Satisfaction

This MO is abundantly satisfied when the witnesses would say: **this domain is attended without being managed.** The artifex's relationship to this domain has shifted from carrying it as mental load to trusting the system holds it. Follow-through happens. Nothing important falls through. The system feels like an extension of the artifex's own awareness — not another thing demanding attention.

The ceiling is absence of concern, not presence of perfection. Improvements the artifex wouldn't think of unprompted mean the MO is already satisfied — stop. Loose ends the artifex would be surprised by mean it isn't — keep going.
